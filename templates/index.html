Frequently asked questions

Short answers to what people ask us first.

What industries do you serve?

We work with healthcare providers, financial institutions, educational organisations, retail businesses and manufacturers. Each solution is tailored to the needs of that industry.

How do you ensure quality?

We use automated testing, manual QA and peer code reviews throughout development. Every project goes through several quality checkpoints before delivery.

Do you provide ongoing maintenance?

Yes. Our maintenance plans include updates, security patches, performance monitoring and technical support with guaranteed response times.

How do you protect client data?

We use encryption, access controls and regular security audits, and we follow data protection regulations to keep your information secure.

Do you offer domain registration and hosting?

Yes. We provide domain registration and hosting with competitive pricing and reliable performance, so your website is online quickly.

What is the typical project timeline?

Simple websites and prototypes take 2 to 4 weeks. Full web applications and complex systems usually take 2 to 6 months, depending on scope.
